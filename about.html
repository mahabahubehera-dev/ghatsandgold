Our Story

Rooted in the Eastern Ghats

In the misty hills of Kandhamal, Odisha, where ancient traditions meet pure nature, our turmeric — and everything we make since — is grown with care by local farmers.

A Promise to the Farmer, First

Ghats & Gold began with a simple belief: the people who grow our food deserve a fair, direct share of its value — not a shrinking cut at the end of a long chain.

Every partnership is direct, every batch is traceable, and every rupee of premium we earn is shared back with the growers of Kandhamal, Koraput and Similipal.
